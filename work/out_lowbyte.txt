CONCLUSION
==============================================================================
Every out-of-range f8 has a low byte in 0..17 with a high part that is a
multiple of 0x100, so f8 is (tag << 8) | root_index.  Reading the low
byte when the whole value is not a valid index yields a root for all
734 symbols that would otherwise emit <harmony> with no <root>.
